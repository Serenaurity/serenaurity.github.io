## 2026-09-10 refinement

The original chunky vCard layout was refined into a slimmer reading layout: a 258px profile sidebar, a wider content panel, lighter 1px separators, compact skill rows, detailed projects without modals or cover thumbnails, and an al-folio-inspired two-column public repository list. The visual identity remains dark, gold, and Poppins. The contact form is intentionally untested for delivery; it requires FormSubmit recipient activation and a hosted or local HTTP preview.
